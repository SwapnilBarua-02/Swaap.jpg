Who I Am

Hey! I’m Swapnil — a creator, and someone who finds joy in storytelling through images. Whether it’s light, shadows, or fleeting emotions, I try to capture what words sometimes can’t.

What I Do

I explore and try to capture the moments and things that goes unnoticed . My photography blends minimalism with mood — it’s about honesty and emotion rather than perfection. Every photo I take is a reflection of how I see and feel the world at that moment.
