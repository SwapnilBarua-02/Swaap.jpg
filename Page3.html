Behind the Scenes

Before & After — exploring light, tone, and emotion.

Adjusted the horizon grid to pull the frame slightly lower it grounded the sea and let the warm edge light breathe across the rocks. Sometimes, balance is more about restraint than correction.

Shot handheld through the arch, aligning the rail pattern with the Opera House sails. The geometry tightened the story — the negative space did most of the talking.

Switched to manual focus to control depth, the autofocus kept chasing movement in the grass. Wide open at f/1.8, the bokeh turned chaos into softness.

Dropped exposure slightly and shifted white balance toward warmth. The orange leaves started sculpting their own shadows. It’s where texture met tone, and fall found its colour rhythm.

Underexposed by a stop to hold the highlights, the lines of flame stayed clean without clipping. The repetition created a rhythm the sensor couldn’t feel but the eye could.
